## Frontend UI/UX Design System Mandate (Antigravity & Light Theme)
- **Palette:** Light Cream / Warm White (`#FAF8F5`, `#F5F2EB`, `#FFFFFF`, charcoal slate text `#0F172A`, accented with electric indigo, purple, and emerald).
- **Libraries:** Always utilize **GSAP (GreenSock)** + **ScrollTrigger**, **Lenis Smooth Scroll**, and **Framer Motion**.
- **Design Philosophy:** Antigravity 3D physics (`SKILL.md`) — floating spatial depth, diffused soft shadows, subtle glassmorphism with light refraction, isometric perspective matrices, and zero static/boring components.
