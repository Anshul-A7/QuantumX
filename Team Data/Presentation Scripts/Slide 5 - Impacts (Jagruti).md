### **IMPACTS — QuantumX**

Now, let us look at the **real-world impact of QuantumX, and who it impacts the most.**

Our primary target is **healthcare professionals**, especially lab technicians, pathologists, and doctors.

Today, patient data goes through laboratory testing and, where available, AI-based analysis before the healthcare professional reviews the case. But difficult or borderline cases still need closer attention and expert review. 

QuantumX adds a **hybrid classical-quantum layer** to this workflow, helping detect complex patterns and flag cases that need a closer look.

- *For Healthcare Professionals & Patients:* It gives professionals better support to find difficult cases earlier, helping patients get faster attention and referral.

- *For Healthcare & Researchers:* It improves the screening process and gives researchers a platform to compare Classical ML and Quantum ML on the same medical data.

- *For Hospitals & Clinics:* It makes medical testing far more dependable by catching cases that are normally overlooked, and is lightweight enough to run without slowing down daily hospital work.

- *For Economies & Insurance:* It reduces long-term healthcare costs by catching diseases early, preventing expensive emergency surgeries and chronic care.


**In simple terms, QuantumX does not replace the doctor or the existing system. It adds another layer of intelligence between the data and the final decision.**
